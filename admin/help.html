How to update the Shan-D website

You can change the website using a simple online editor. Sign in with GitHub, open the section you want, make your change, then save.

Open the Shan-D editor ↗

Change music in the shop

  1. Choose 1. Music shop, then Your music catalogue.
  2. Click a release to open it. Change its name, price, description, Bandcamp link or cover.
  3. To add a song, choose Add an item. Fill in the boxes and paste its Bandcamp page into Bandcamp checkout page.
  4. To remove a release, open it and choose the remove option.
  5. Save your change. Your public website will update after it publishes.

Change show dates

  1. Choose 2. Upcoming shows, then Your show dates.
  2. Choose Add an item. Enter the date like 2026-12-06, the venue and the town or city.
  3. Paste a ticket link if tickets are on sale. Leave it blank if they are not.
  4. Save. Remove a show after it has happened or if it is cancelled.

Change your website info

  1. Choose 3. Website info to edit your About text, social links and mailing-list details.
  2. For a social link, enter its name and the full web address, starting with https://.
  3. The mailing-list box starts off disconnected. Choose an email service first, then copy its sign-up form details into the mailing-list boxes. The service must give you permission to collect and email your fans.
  4. Save. The mailing list will only collect addresses after an email service is connected.

Remember

  • Use the same GitHub account that owns the Shan-D website repository to sign in.
  • Check Bandcamp links carefully. Bandcamp handles payment and music downloads.
  • Never put a password, PayPal login or bank details into an editor box.

Your first time opening the editor

The Pages CMS website needs access to the Shan-D website repository to show the edit boxes and save changes. Choose only the Shan-D website repository when GitHub asks what Pages CMS can access.

  1. Open the editor and choose Sign in with GitHub.
  2. Allow the Pages CMS GitHub app to access Shan-DUK/shan-duk.github.io.
  3. Return to Pages CMS and open the Shan-D repository.

If the repository does not appear, use the Pages CMS app installation screen to add access for that one repository. The website will publish your saved edits automatically.
