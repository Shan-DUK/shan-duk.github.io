How to update the Shan-D website

You can change the website using a simple online editor. Sign in with GitHub, open the section you want, make your change, then save.

Open the Shan-D editor ↗

Change music in the shop

  1. Choose 1. Music shop, then Your music catalogue.
  2. Click a release to open it. Change its name, price, description, Bandcamp link or cover.
  3. To add a song, choose Add an item. Fill in the boxes and paste its Bandcamp page into Bandcamp checkout page.
  4. To remove a release, open it and choose the remove option.
  5. Save your change. Your public website will update after it publishes.

Change show dates

  1. Choose 2. Upcoming shows, then Your show dates.
  2. Choose Add an item. Enter the date like 2026-12-06, the venue and the town or city.
  3. Paste a ticket link if tickets are on sale. Leave it blank if they are not.
  4. Save. Remove a show after it has happened or if it is cancelled.

Add or change a mix

  1. Choose 3. Mixes, then Your mixes.
  2. Choose Add an item. Enter the mix name and, if you like, a short note.
  3. In How should this mix play?, choose SoundCloud link or Upload an audio file.
  4. For SoundCloud, paste the full track or playlist link in SoundCloud link. Public links and share links work.
  5. For your own player, choose the audio file from your computer in Upload a mix from your computer. MP3 is recommended and works on the most devices.
  6. Only fill in the box for the option you chose. Save. The mix will appear on the Mixes page when the site publishes.

Who can listen? SoundCloud share links and uploaded audio are placed on the public website. Anyone who visits the site can listen, so only add mixes you want everyone to hear.

Change your website info

  1. Choose 4. Website info to edit your About text, photos, social links and mailing-list details.
  2. To change a photo, open Photos of Shan-D and pick the photo. To add one, choose Add an item and select a photo from your computer.
  3. Write a short caption and describe what is in the photo. Add an Instagram link only if you want people to open that post when they click the photo.
  4. For a social link, enter its name and the full web address, starting with https://.
  5. The sign-up form is shown on the homepage. It asks fans for an email address and permission to receive occasional updates.
  6. Google Forms keeps the sign-ups in Shan-D’s Google account. Open Shan-D Mailing List Sign-up, then choose Responses. The private Fan Mailing List spreadsheet will keep a simple table of sign-ups.
  7. When you want an email sent, ask ChatGPT to write it and send it to your mailing list. Before sending, it checks for unsubscribe requests and hides subscriber addresses from each other.
  8. Every email should tell fans how to leave the list. Replying UNSUBSCRIBE is the current method.
  9. If you replace the form, paste its full Google Forms responder link into the mailing-list sign-up box, then save. The website will show the new form.

Who can see the photos? They appear on the public website. Only add pictures you are happy for everyone to see.

Remember

  • Use the same GitHub account that owns the Shan-D website repository to sign in.
  • Check Bandcamp links carefully. Bandcamp handles payment and music downloads.
  • Never put a password, PayPal login or bank details into an editor box.

Your first time opening the editor

The Pages CMS website needs access to the Shan-D website repository to show the edit boxes and save changes. Choose only the Shan-D website repository when GitHub asks what Pages CMS can access.

  1. Open the editor and choose Sign in with GitHub.
  2. Allow the Pages CMS GitHub app to access Shan-DUK/shan-duk.github.io.
  3. Return to Pages CMS and open the Shan-D repository.

If the repository does not appear, use the Pages CMS app installation screen to add access for that one repository. The website will publish your saved edits automatically.
